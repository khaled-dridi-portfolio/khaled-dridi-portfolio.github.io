A comprehensive mod for Minecraft Bedrock Edition introducing three new mob entities with unique AI behaviors and environmental interactions. Features include the Lava Spider with projectile attacks and lava swimming mechanics, the Drowned Spider with water-walking abilities and status effect application, and the Firefly with autonomous flight patterns and dynamic lighting effects.
